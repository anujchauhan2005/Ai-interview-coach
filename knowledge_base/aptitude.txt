== APTITUDE & HR QUESTIONS ==

--- BEHAVIORAL QUESTIONS (STAR Method) ---
STAR = Situation → Task → Action → Result

"Tell me about yourself":
Structure: Current role/background → key skills/experience → why this role
Keep it 90 seconds. Focus on professional journey, not personal life.

"Why do you want to work here?":
- Research the company's product, culture, tech stack, recent news
- Connect their mission to your career goals
- Mention specific things: "I love that you use Python/ML for X"

"Strengths":
- Pick strengths relevant to the JD
- Give a real example: "I'm strong in data analysis — at my internship I reduced reporting time by 30%"

"Weaknesses":
- Pick a real weakness that is NOT critical to the role
- Show you're actively working on it
- Example: "I used to struggle with presenting to stakeholders, so I started doing weekly team demos"

"Where do you see yourself in 5 years?":
- Show ambition but also commitment to the role
- "I want to grow into a senior data analyst / ML engineer role, and I see this position as the perfect foundation"

"Tell me about a challenge you faced":
- Use STAR, make it professional
- Focus on your specific actions, not the team's
- End with what you learned

"Tell me about a time you failed":
- Show self-awareness and learning
- Don't blame others
- "I missed a deadline on X because I underestimated the complexity. I learned to break tasks into smaller milestones"

"Why should we hire you?":
- 3 reasons: skills match, experience match, cultural fit
- Be specific with numbers and examples

"Do you prefer working alone or in a team?":
- "I can do both. I prefer collaborative environments but am productive independently too"

--- HR QUESTIONS ---

"What is your expected salary?":
- Research market rate on Glassdoor/AmbitionBox first
- Give a range: "Based on my research and my skills, I'm looking for X-Y LPA, but I'm open to discussion"

"Are you comfortable with relocation?":
- Be honest. If yes, say so clearly.

"How do you handle pressure?":
- Give a specific example
- Mention tools: prioritization, breaking into smaller tasks, communicating early

"What motivates you?":
- Problem-solving, learning new things, seeing impact of your work
- Connect to the role

"Tell me about a time you worked in a team":
- Use STAR, describe your specific role and contribution
- Mention conflict resolution if any

--- APTITUDE TOPICS ---

Time & Work:
- If A can do work in x days, rate = 1/x per day
- Combined rate = 1/x + 1/y, then time = xy/(x+y)

Speed, Distance, Time:
- Distance = Speed × Time
- Average speed = 2xy/(x+y) for same distance at two speeds
- Relative speed: same direction = difference, opposite = sum

Percentages:
- % change = (New-Old)/Old × 100
- Successive % change: a% then b% = a + b + ab/100

Profit & Loss:
- Profit % = (Profit/CP) × 100
- SP = CP × (1 + profit%/100)
- Marked Price, Discount = MP - SP

Ratios & Proportions:
- a:b = c:d → a×d = b×c (cross multiply)
- Compound ratio: multiply ratios

Mixtures & Alligation:
- Rule of Alligation: cheap:expensive = (expensive-mean):(mean-cheap)

Permutations & Combinations:
- nPr = n!/(n-r)! (order matters)
- nCr = n!/(r!(n-r)!) (order doesn't matter)
- Circular permutation: (n-1)!

Probability:
- P(A) = favorable outcomes / total outcomes
- P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B) for independent events
- P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A and B)

Number Systems:
- Binary to Decimal: multiply by powers of 2
- Decimal to Binary: successive division by 2
- HCF & LCM: HCF × LCM = product of two numbers
- Divisibility rules: 2(even), 3(digit sum div by 3), 5(ends 0/5), 9(digit sum div by 9)

Series & Sequences:
- Arithmetic: a, a+d, a+2d... Sum = n/2 × (2a + (n-1)d)
- Geometric: a, ar, ar²... Sum = a(r^n - 1)/(r-1)

--- COMPANY-SPECIFIC PATTERNS ---

Product Startups (Flipkart, Swiggy, Zomato, Razorpay):
- Heavy DSA focus (LeetCode Medium/Hard)
- System design for senior roles
- Strong behavioral emphasis on ownership and impact

IT Services (TCS, Infosys, Wipro):
- Aptitude (quantitative, verbal, logical reasoning)
- OOP concepts
- DBMS and SQL
- Basic data structures
- Communication skills

FAANG/Big Tech (Google, Microsoft, Amazon):
- Hard DSA problems
- System design (Distributed systems, databases, caching)
- Behavioral (Leadership Principles for Amazon)
- Code quality and testing

Analytics/BI roles:
- SQL (complex queries, window functions, optimization)
- Excel / Power BI / Tableau
- Statistics (mean, median, standard deviation, regression)
- Python (Pandas, NumPy)
- Problem-solving with data

--- CODING INTERVIEW TIPS ---
1. Clarify the problem before coding (edge cases, constraints, expected output)
2. Think out loud — explain your approach
3. Start with brute force, then optimize
4. Discuss time and space complexity
5. Write clean code with meaningful variable names
6. Test with examples including edge cases (empty, single element, negative numbers)
7. Practice on LeetCode: Easy (warm up) → Medium (target) → Hard (stretch)
